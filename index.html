Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026  ·  App: Clean Game Turbo (com.cleangameturbo.pro)

This Privacy Policy explains how the Clean Game Turbo app ("App", "we") handles data when you use it. The App is designed so that most data processing happens directly on your device — we do not operate our own backend servers to store your personal data.

1. Data and Permissions Used by the App

Below are the permissions the App actually requests, and exactly what each one is used for:

Media Access (Photos / Videos / Audio)

Used by the Storage Analyzer feature to calculate and categorize file sizes (photos, videos, audio) stored on your device, so you can see a breakdown of your storage usage. The App does not upload, copy, or transmit your media files to any server — all scanning happens locally on your device. The App does not include a cache/file-cleaning feature.

Usage Access

Used to detect when a game you've selected is running in the foreground, so the Game Booster feature (per-game performance mode) can activate automatically.

Notification Access (Notification Listener)

If you enable the "Block Notifications" option for a game, the App uses this permission only to hide/cancel notifications that appear while that game session is running. The App does not read your notification content for any other purpose, does not store it, and does not send it to anyone.

List of Installed Apps

Used solely to display a list of apps/games installed on your device on the "Add Games" screen, so you can choose which games to optimize.

Display Over Other Apps, Notifications, Wake Lock, Foreground Service

Used to run the Game Booster overlay, keep the optimization process running during a game session, and show the foreground-service notification required by Android for this type of feature.

Internet Access & Network State

Used to display ads (see Section 3) and to process VIP purchases through Google Play Billing.

We do not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, or call logs. The App also does not require account creation or login — no name, email address, or phone number is collected by the App itself.

2. Data Stored Locally

The following preferences are stored only in local storage on your device (not sent to our servers):

  • Per-game performance settings (Auto Mode, CPU cooling, RAM cleaner, notification blocking)
  • VIP membership status (after verification through Google Play Billing)
  • Storage scan history and other display preferences

This data is automatically deleted if you uninstall the App.

4. Data Security

Because most data is processed and stored locally on your device, the risk of a data breach on our end is minimal. We still follow reasonable development practices to maintain the integrity of the App.

5. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Your Rights

Since the App does not store personal data on our servers, the most effective way to remove all App-related data is to uninstall the App from your device. If you have questions about how the App processes data, please contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when new features are added. Changes will be reflected by the "Effective date" at the top of this page.
