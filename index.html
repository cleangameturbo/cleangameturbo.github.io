01Overview

Clean Game Turbo provides game‑session tools (Game Mode overlay, Game Booster), a storage and junk cleaner, photo/video/audio cleanup tools, and device‑info screens. Most of what the app does — scanning storage, showing device stats, applying booster settings — happens entirely on your device and is never sent to us.

The app does show ads through Google AdMob and sells a VIP subscription through Google Play Billing, and those two services do collect some data on their own behalf, as described below. This policy tells you exactly which permissions unlock which feature, and what each one does and doesn't do with your data.

02Data we collect

Media you choose to clean up

When you open the Photo, Video, or Audio cleaner, the app reads your media library on‑device to find junk, duplicates, and large files, and shows you what it found so you can decide what to delete. Files are never uploaded, copied off your device, or transmitted to us or anyone else.

Notifications, during Game Mode only

If you grant Notification Access, it is used solely to apply Do Not Disturb / notification‑filtering behavior while a Game Mode session you started is active. The app does not read, log, store, or transmit the content of your notifications.

Foreground app signal

The Usage Access permission lets the app detect which app is currently in the foreground, so features like the game picker and the Game Mode watchdog can recognize when a game you've configured is running. This is processed on‑device in real time and is not recorded into a history or sent to us.

Device and performance stats

CPU, RAM, battery, temperature, and network information shown in Device Info, Battery, and Network screens is read from the Android system to display to you. It is not transmitted off your device by us.

Advertising and installed‑app identifiers

Google AdMob (and, where required, Google's User Messaging Platform for consent) collects an advertising identifier and device information to show and measure ads. A scoped app‑query is used to list your launchable apps only inside the in‑app game picker; the app does not have broad visibility into every app on your device.

Purchase information

VIP subscription purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation of your subscription status, not your payment card or billing details.

03Permissions we request

Android requires apps to declare each permission up front. Here is what each one unlocks and why the app asks for it.

Permission
What it's for
Photos, videos, audio
Powers the Storage, Photo, Video, and Audio cleaners. Read locally, never uploaded.
Notification access
Applies Do Not Disturb / filtering only during a Game Mode session you start.
Usage access (app stats)
Detects the current foreground app for the game picker and Game Mode watchdog.
Display over other apps
Draws the optional Game Mode performance overlay and crosshair overlay on top of your game.
Modify system settings
Lets Game Booster adjust screen brightness during a session, at your request.

Foreground service, wake lock

Network / Wi‑Fi state
Reads connection status for the Network Info screen and to load ads.

You can review or revoke any of these at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Clean Game Turbo → Permissions. Turning a permission off disables the related feature but does not affect the rest of the app.

04How we use information

  • To run the feature you opened — cleaning, boosting, or displaying device info.
  • To detect the foreground app so Game Mode and the game picker work correctly.
  • To apply notification filtering during a Game Mode session you turned on.
  • To serve and measure ads, and to verify and restore your VIP subscription, through Google's SDKs.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability.

We do not use your media, notifications, or usage data to build an advertising profile of you, and we do not sell that data.

05Third‑party services

Clean Game Turbo relies on the following Google services to operate:

  • Google AdMob — serves banner, interstitial, rewarded, and app‑open ads, and collects advertising identifiers and device data under its own privacy policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — shows a consent form to users in the EEA, UK, and other regions that require it, before any ad request is made.
  • Google Play Billing — processes VIP subscription purchases and renewals.
Note: Data handled by these Google services is governed by Google's own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

06Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the third‑party services listed above, strictly so they can perform the function you're using (showing an ad, processing a purchase, gathering consent) — and, if ever required, to comply with a valid legal request.

07Retention & storage

Most data the app touches — media metadata, device stats, usage‑access signals — is processed in memory on your device and is not retained by us in any form. Local app preferences (such as your Game Mode settings per game and VIP status cache) are stored on your device and removed when you uninstall the app. Data collected by Google's SDKs is retained according to Google's own retention policies.

08Your choices

  • Permissions: grant or revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings.
  • Ad personalization: reset or opt out of your advertising ID under Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Consent (EEA/UK): reopen the privacy‑options / consent form from the app's Settings screen at any time.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel VIP through the Google Play Store subscriptions page.
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes all locally stored app data from your device.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA). Contact us using the details below to make a request.

09Children's privacy

Clean Game Turbo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

10Security

We rely on Android's standard app sandboxing and permission model, and on the security practices of Google's SDKs, to protect data the app handles. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11International transfers

Where Google's services process data outside your country (for ads, billing, or consent), that transfer is governed by Google's own safeguards and privacy policy.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app's features change. Updates take effect as soon as they're posted here, and the "Effective date" above will reflect the latest revision. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
