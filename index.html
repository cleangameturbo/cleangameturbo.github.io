Privacy Policy – Clean Game Turbo

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clean Game Turbo (“the App”), published by Kamal tech, handles information when you use the App.

1. Information and data handled by the App

Clean Game Turbo is designed to work primarily on your Android device. The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, address, or other profile information to use its core features.

Depending on the features you use, the App may access or process:

  • Device information needed to display CPU frequency, battery level, battery temperature, volume, brightness, or other device status information.
  • Information about games that you choose to add to the App, such as the game name and package identifier, so the App can launch the selected game and apply your saved settings.
  • Photos, videos, audio, and other media information when you explicitly use the Cleaner/Storage features to review or delete selected media.
  • Information required to operate Android overlay, notification, Do Not Disturb, screen, and system-setting features when you grant the corresponding Android permissions.

2. Advertising

The App may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may process device or advertising information according to their own policies and the choices available through the App and Android/Google settings.

Where required, the App uses Google's consent mechanisms to request and manage consent for personalized advertising.

3. In-app purchases and subscriptions

VIP subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. The App does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Purchase and subscription information is processed by Google Play to complete purchases, restore purchases, and verify your VIP entitlement.

4. Permissions

The App may request Android permissions or special access only when a feature requires them. Depending on your use of the App, these may include:

  • Display over other apps – for the optional in-game Game Mode overlay.
  • Notification access – for notification-related Game Mode features when enabled by you.
  • Do Not Disturb access – for Focus/Silent Notification features when enabled by you.
  • Modify system settings – for supported brightness, volume, or related controls.
  • Storage/media access – for Cleaner and media-management features.
  • Notifications – for App notifications where enabled.
  • Foreground service access – to keep the user-started Game Mode session and overlay running while an active gaming session is in progress.

You can deny optional permissions, although the related feature may not work.

5. How information is used

Information accessed on the device is used to provide the feature you requested, such as launching a selected game, showing device status, controlling supported settings, displaying an overlay, or helping you review and manage storage/media.

The App does not use device information to modify the code, memory, or internal files of another game, and it does not provide tools intended to cheat or gain an unfair gameplay advantage.

6. Data sharing

The App does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the App, including Google services used for advertising, consent management, and Google Play purchases. Android system components may also process information according to your device settings and Google's/Android's applicable policies.

7. Data retention

Settings and locally required app data may remain on your device until you change them, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Purchase entitlement information may be synchronized with Google Play when needed to restore or verify your subscription.

8. Children's privacy

Clean Game Turbo is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the App, please contact us using the contact information below.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to limit access to information used by the App. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Third-party services

The App may rely on Google services such as Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads, and Google's consent technology. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies and terms.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its features, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will show a new effective date.
