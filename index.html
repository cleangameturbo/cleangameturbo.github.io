Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Clean Game Turbo ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our Android application. We built the App to do as much as possible directly on your device, and we do not require you to create an account to use it.

2. Information We Access

The table below lists the device permissions the App may request and what each one is used for. Unless stated otherwise, this information stays on your device and is not collected, uploaded, or sold by us.

Permission Purpose
Photos, videos & audio files (media access) Used locally by the Storage & Junk Cleaner to analyze storage usage by category. Files are read on-device only; they are never uploaded or transmitted off your device.
Usage Access (App usage stats) Used to detect which app is currently in the foreground so Game Mode and Game Booster can apply per-game settings. Processed locally; no browsing or usage history is transmitted to us.
Accessibility Service Used, only if you explicitly enable it, to detect the active window/app for Game Mode's automatic overlay behavior. It does not read, log, or transmit on-screen text, keystrokes, or content from other apps.
Display over other apps (overlay) Used to show the in-game performance overlay (CPU, RAM, battery, FPS) during an active Game Mode session.
Modify system settings Used to apply display/performance adjustments you request through Game Booster.
Notifications Used to show status notifications for foreground services (e.g., an active boost or Game Mode session) and app alerts.
Network state / Wi-Fi state / Internet Used to display network status in Device Info, and to load ads and verify VIP purchases, as described below.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require or collect your name, email address, or any account registration information.
  • We do not read, upload, or transmit the contents of your photos, videos, or audio files.
  • We do not log keystrokes, screen content, or on-screen text from other apps, even when the Accessibility Service is enabled.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Advertising

The App displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate location, and app interaction data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

  • Where required (for example, under GDPR in the European Economic Area and UK), the App presents a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before showing personalized ads, and you can choose non-personalized ads or withdraw consent at any time from Settings > Privacy Options.
  • We do not control how Google or its advertising partners use data collected through the ad SDK. You can review Google's practices at their Privacy Policy and manage ad personalization at Google Ads Settings.

5. VIP Subscription & Purchases

VIP purchases are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google may process purchase and account information according to its own Privacy Policy in order to verify and manage your subscription.

6. Data Storage & Retention

  • App preferences, VIP/Game Mode settings, and per-game configurations are stored locally on your device.
  • Storage analysis results and device metrics (CPU, RAM, battery, network) are computed on-device and are not sent to our servers.
  • Uninstalling the App removes locally stored app data from your device.

7. Data Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties, except:

  • With Google AdMob/UMP for ad delivery and consent management, as described in Section 4.
  • With Google Play Billing for subscription processing, as described in Section 5.
  • Where required to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or governmental request.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

9. Your Choices

  • You can grant or revoke any Android permission (storage, usage access, accessibility, overlay, notifications) at any time from your device's Settings app.
  • You can manage ad personalization consent at any time from Settings > Privacy Options within the App.
  • You can manage or cancel your VIP subscription at any time through Google Play > Subscriptions.

10. Security

We rely on Android's platform security model and standard secure development practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting to this page, and we will update the "Last Updated" date above.

12. Contact

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the App, please contact us at support.cleangameturbo@gmail.com.
