Privacy Policy

Clean Game Turbo
Package name: com.cleangameturbo.pro
Effective date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clean Game Turbo ("the App") handles information when you install and use the application.

Summary: Clean Game Turbo does not require you to create an account. The App primarily processes device, storage, gaming, and system information locally on your Android device. Certain third-party services, including Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, and Google Play Billing, may process information according to their own policies.

1. Information We Process

Device Information

Clean Game Turbo may access information provided by the Android operating system, including device model, manufacturer, Android version, RAM, CPU information, storage information, battery information, and other device status information.

This information is used to display device status and provide device optimization and gaming-related features.

Installed Applications and Games

The App may identify applications that are available through the Android launcher so that you can select and manage games inside Clean Game Turbo.

This information is used to provide the game selection and game-management features.

Usage Access

If you voluntarily enable Android Usage Access, Clean Game Turbo may determine which application is currently in the foreground.

This permission is used by features such as Auto Game Mode to recognize when a selected game has been launched.

Usage information is used for the App's functionality and is not intended to be uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Storage and Media

When you grant the applicable Android permissions and use storage or cleaning features, Clean Game Turbo may access files and media available to the App.

Depending on the Android version and permissions granted, this may include information about photos, videos, audio files, downloads, cache files, temporary files, file names, file sizes, and other storage information.

This information is used for storage analysis, duplicate-file detection, cleaning features, and other actions that you explicitly request.

Clean Game Turbo does not intentionally upload your personal photos, videos, audio files, or scanned storage contents to a developer-operated server.

Notification Access

If you voluntarily enable Android Notification Listener access, Clean Game Turbo may receive notification events so that its notification-control feature can suppress or manage notifications during supported gaming modes.

Notification access is used only for the related functionality. Notification content is not intentionally uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Network Information

The App may access network information available through Android, such as connection type, IP-related information, DNS information, Wi-Fi information where permitted by Android, link speed, signal-related information, and network latency.

This information is used to display network status and provide network-related features.

Gaming Sessions

Clean Game Turbo may store gaming-session information locally on your device. This may include the selected game, start time, end time, session duration, and statistics calculated by the App.

2. Advertising

The free version of Clean Game Turbo may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Clean Game Turbo also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where applicable to manage advertising consent.

Google may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and other information according to Google's applicable policies and your consent choices.

For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Policies and Technologies

3. Google Play Billing and VIP

Clean Game Turbo offers optional VIP subscriptions through Google Play Billing.

Available subscription plans may include weekly and monthly subscriptions. Prices shown to users are provided by Google Play and may vary depending on country, currency, taxes, promotions, and account eligibility.

Payment information is processed by Google Play. Clean Game Turbo does not ask you to provide your credit card number, bank account credentials, or other payment credentials directly to the App.

The App may receive purchase and subscription status information from Google Play in order to provide or remove VIP access.

4. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, Clean Game Turbo may request Android permissions or special access including:

  • Internet access for network-related functions and third-party services.
  • Storage or media access for storage analysis and cleaning features.
  • Notification permission for App notifications.
  • Notification Listener access for notification-control features.
  • Usage Access for Auto Game Mode and foreground-game detection.
  • Display over other apps for supported gaming overlay features.
  • Do Not Disturb or related system access for supported gaming-mode features.
  • System settings access for supported device-setting features.
  • Foreground service access for features that need to continue operating while the App is not in the foreground.
  • Wake lock functionality for supported gaming operations.

You can deny or revoke optional permissions through Android settings. Some App features may stop working when their required permissions are denied.

5. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

  • We do not require a Clean Game Turbo account.
  • We do not intentionally collect passwords.
  • We do not intentionally collect contact lists.
  • We do not directly collect payment-card information.
  • We do not intentionally upload your personal photos, videos, or audio files to a developer-operated server.
  • We do not intentionally sell your personal information.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Information used by Clean Game Turbo for device settings, game profiles, gaming sessions, and similar functionality may be stored locally on your Android device.

Local information may remain until you remove it through the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

Third-party services such as Google AdMob, Google UMP, and Google Play may retain information according to their own policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to limit unnecessary collection and transmission of information. However, no application, device, network, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Clean Game Turbo is not specifically directed toward children. The App does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to the developer.

If you believe that personal information has been improperly provided to the developer, please contact us using the contact information below.

  • Google Play Billing

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.
