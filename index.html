Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Clean Game Turbo ("the App", "we") handles information when you use the App. Clean Game Turbo has no backend server and no user accounts — the App does not operate any database or server that stores your personal data. Everything the App reads about your device is processed locally, on your device, except where explicitly noted below for advertising and billing, which are handled by Google's own services.

3. Advertising

Free-tier use of the App is supported by ads served through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, app-open, and rewarded ad formats). Google may collect an advertising identifier and other data as described in Google's Partner Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

For users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent before any ad is requested, in line with GDPR. You can review or withdraw your ad consent choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Options inside the App.

VIP subscribers do not see ads — subscribing through Google Play Billing disables all ad formats in the App in addition to unlocking Game Mode.

4. Purchases & Billing

VIP subscription purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The App does not collect, see, or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.) — that information is handled directly between you and Google Play. The App only receives a purchase/entitlement status from Google's Billing Library to unlock VIP features.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require or use a user account, sign-in, or profile.
  • We do not upload, back up, or transmit your photos, videos, or audio files to any server.
  • We do not read the content of your notifications — only the sending app's package name, and only during an active, user-enabled Game Mode session.
  • We do not sell your data.

6. Data Retention & Security

Because the App has no backend, there is no remotely stored copy of your data to retain or delete on request — uninstalling the App removes all of its local settings and cache. Media you deleted through the Cleaner is removed by Android itself and cannot be recovered by us or by you.

7. Children's Privacy

Clean Game Turbo is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below.

8. Your Choices

  • Any permission listed in §2 can be revoked at any time from Android Settings → Apps → Clean Game Turbo → Permissions; the App will simply be unable to perform the related feature until re-granted.
  • Ad consent can be reviewed or withdrawn anytime via Settings → Privacy Options in the App.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all of its locally stored settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect as soon as they are posted to this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
