Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clean Game Turbo ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use the App. Clean Game Turbo is a game performance and utility app for Android. We built it to do as much as possible on your device, without sending your personal data to us.

1. Summary

  • We do not operate our own server that stores your personal data.
  • Game Mode, Game Booster, and Privacy Mode all run entirely on your device.
  • Privacy Mode's local-only VPN never sends your traffic to a remote server, and we cannot see or log it.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob and offers a VIP subscription through Google Play Billing; both are handled by Google, not by us directly.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

The App is designed around on-device functionality. The table below lists each permission or data category the App uses, why it's needed, and where that data goes.

Permission / Data Purpose Leaves the device?
Installed / launchable apps list Populate the "Add Games" picker and per-game settings screens. No
Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) Detect which app is currently in the foreground, so Game Mode/Booster actions apply to the right game and restore correctly when you leave it. No
Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) Show the optional Game Mode performance overlay (CPU, RAM, temperature, battery, FPS) on top of a game. No

Do Not Disturb access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY) Turn Do Not Disturb on while boosting a game and restore your previous setting afterward. No

VPN service (Privacy Mode) Create a local-only tunnel that blocks internet access only for the specific apps you select (e.g. chat apps), so they can't use data while you're gaming. No — no remote VPN server is used; traffic for non-selected apps, including your games, is unaffected.
Media files (images/video/audio, or user-selected media on Android 14+) Used only for features that require you to pick a file on-device (e.g. a custom image), if and when you use them. No
Network state / Wi-Fi state Check connectivity status for network-dependent features (e.g. ads, VIP status checks). No
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) Show the required persistent notification while a foreground service (Game Mode/Booster session or Privacy Mode) is running, as required by Android. No

Purchase/subscription status Google Play Billing verifies and manages your VIP subscription. Yes — to Google Play only. See Section 4.

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contact list to operate the App's core features.

3. How Game Mode, Booster, and Privacy Mode Handle Data

  • Game Mode: Per-game settings (on/off, which stats to show) are stored locally in the App's private storage on your device. Nothing about which games you play or when is sent to us.
  • Game Booster: RAM/CPU optimization, brightness, volume, and DND actions are performed locally using Android system APIs and are reversed automatically when you leave the game or turn the relevant setting off.
  • Privacy Mode (VPN): Uses Android's built-in VpnService to run a local-only tunnel entirely on your device. We do not run a remote VPN server, and we do not transmit, store, or log your network traffic. Only apps you explicitly select are affected; everything else, including your games, keeps its normal connection.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — serves ads within the App. In the EEA, UK, and other regions that require it, we show a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before requesting personalized ads, and you can review or change your choice from the App's settings. See Google's Partner Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes VIP subscription purchases and renewals. We never see or store your payment details; Google Play manages billing and refunds directly.

We do not control, and are not responsible for, how these third parties handle data once it reaches them. We encourage you to review Google's privacy policy for details.

5. Data Storage & Retention

App settings (per-game Game Mode configuration, VIP status cache, Privacy Mode app selection, and similar preferences) are stored locally on your device using standard Android private storage and are removed automatically when you uninstall the App. We do not maintain a backend database of user data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Your Choices

  • Every permission described in Section 2 (overlay, usage access, notification access, DND access, VPN, media access) is optional and can be granted, revoked, or reset at any time from Android Settings or the App's own Settings screen.
  • You can turn off Game Mode, Booster features, or Privacy Mode per game at any time.
  • You can manage or revoke ad personalization consent from the App's Privacy Options, where available.
  • You can cancel your VIP subscription at any time through Google Play.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored settings.

8. Data Security

We rely on Android's app-sandboxing and permission model to protect data stored by the App on your device. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but because most App functionality is on-device, the amount of data that could be exposed to us or in transit is minimal by design.
