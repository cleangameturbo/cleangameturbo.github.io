02Description of service

Clean Game Turbo is a performance‑utility app for Android built around four tools:

Game Mode
A manual, per‑game overlay showing CPU, RAM, temperature, battery, and FPS during a session. VIP‑only.
Game Booster
CPU/RAM management, notification filtering, and display adjustments while you game.
Storage & junk cleaner
Clears the App's own cache and analyzes storage by photos, videos, and audio.
Device info
Read‑only battery, network, and device screens sourced from Android.

04Game Mode

  • Game Mode is a VIP‑only feature — not available on the free tier.
  • It is completely manual: it never activates automatically based on game detection.
  • It is per‑game: each game has independent settings you enable individually.
  • Settings are not retained across reboots — Game Mode won't resume automatically after a device restart or app relaunch.
  • When disabled for a game, the overlay won't appear even if that game is opened.
Heads up: the overlay displays its own render rate, not the actual FPS of your game, and is provided as‑is for reference only.

05Game Booster

Game Booster applies device‑level performance and notification settings for the duration of a session you start. Results depend on your device model, Android version, and background app load, and are not guaranteed to produce any specific performance improvement.

06Storage & media cleaner

  • Storage and media analysis runs locally on your device; files are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.
  • Deleted cache, junk, photo, video, or audio files cannot be recovered once removed — review carefully before confirming a deletion.
