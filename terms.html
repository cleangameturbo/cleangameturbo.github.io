2. What the App does

Clean Game Turbo is an Android game launcher and device utility. Its current features include:

  • Game list and launch: add launchable apps to the list and open a selected app.
  • Game-session settings: apply selected device settings such as screen brightness, media/ringtone volume, and Do Not Disturb, subject to Android permissions and device support.
  • Game Mode overlay: a per-game, VIP feature that can display the App’s control panel over a game after you enable it and grant overlay permission. The panel may show device-level information and includes an optional control to keep the display awake while the overlay is active.
  • Auto Game Mode: an optional VIP feature that detects a registered game entering the foreground when enabled and when Usage Access is granted.
  • Gaming history and statistics: locally recorded session duration, launch count, battery readings, battery-temperature readings, and system-wide RAM-use readings. These are not measurements of the game’s own FPS, CPU/GPU use, or memory allocation.
  • Cleaner: review media and app-owned temporary files, find large or duplicate media, and choose items for deletion. The App may also show launchable apps and open Android’s standard app-uninstall flow.
  • Device information: view selected hardware, battery, storage, and network information reported by Android.
  • Service notifications: Android may display the App’s own notification while a foreground service is active, subject to Android notification settings and permission.

The App does not modify game code, report in-game FPS, tune a game’s CPU/GPU, or free RAM or clear private cache belonging to another app. Displayed device readings are system-level readings.

3. Permissions and system settings

Some features require permissions or special access that you grant through Android, including access to selected media, Usage Access, Notification Access, overlay permission, permission to modify system settings, or Do Not Disturb access. You can decline or revoke access in Android Settings; the related feature may then be unavailable or limited.

When you enable session controls, changes such as brightness or volume can affect the device outside the selected game. The App’s current session cleanup attempts to restore saved brightness and volume values. For Do Not Disturb, the current code sets Android’s interruption filter to “All” when a session ends; it does not restore a previous custom filter. Check your device’s Do Not Disturb setting after a session if you had a custom filter enabled. Android behavior can vary by version and device.

The optional screen-awake control works while the Game Mode overlay is active and may use additional battery power.

4. Cleaner and deletion

  • The App may directly remove cache and selected temporary files owned by Clean Game Turbo. It does not clear another app’s private cache or RAM.

  • Duplicate detection compares media locally on the device. The App does not upload scanned media to a developer-operated server.
  • The App has no undo for a completed deletion. Android or your media app may or may not provide a recovery option; the App cannot guarantee recovery.
  • If you choose to uninstall another app, the App opens Android’s uninstall flow. Android controls the confirmation and uninstall operation.
